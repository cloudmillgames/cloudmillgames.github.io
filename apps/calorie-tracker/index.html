Calorie Tracker

Calorie Tracker does one task and does it well: track your calories daily consumption, your weight target and changes, and suggest a daily calories limit based on a strategy of your choosing.

Calorie Tracker is designed by humans for humans. Contains many small conscious design decisions to lighten the pressure of following a calorie-counting diet and includes vocal affirmations that act as small positive rewards whenever you log calories today.

Calorie Tracker does not collect nor share any data personal or otherwise, it’s designed with respect for your privacy. Its focus is adding value to your everyday lifestyle.

Calorie Tracker keeps the daily log simple.
Open the app, add an estimate of what you eat, see where you stand against today’s limit. Repeat daily. Simple as that. No food database to search, an educated estimate is all you need.

Add in kCal or kJ
Enter calories in either unit. Convert between them quickly using the built-in nifty little converter.

A daily limit that reacts to you
Set a target weight and choose how you want to get there. Calorie Tracker calculates a daily limit from your height, weight, age, sex and activity level, then recalculates it every day and at every weekly check-in as your weight changes.

Weekly check-in
Once a week, record your weight and how active you are. Your limit updates and the check-in history keeps a running record of your progress.

Your day, your reset time
If your days end early or after mid-night, set your day to end at the time of your choosing and those calories count toward the day they belong to.

Reminders that fit your week
A day-start reminder with today’s limit, and a weekly check-in reminder on whichever day you choose.

Voice encouragement
Optional spoken encouragement when you save an entry. Toggle it on/off whenever you like.

Private by design
Everything stays on your phone. Calorie Tracker has no account, no servers, and works entirely locally.

About the numbers
Calorie and BMI figures are estimates based on standard formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor). Calorie Tracker does not provide medical advice. Speak to a doctor before starting a weight change program.
