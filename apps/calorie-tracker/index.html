Calorie Tracker

Calorie Tracker is an app that does one task and does it well: track your calories consumption, your weight target and changes, and suggest a daily calorie limit based on a strategy of your choosing.

Calorie Tracker is designed by humans for humans. Contains many small conscious design decisions to lighten the pressure of following a calorie-counting diet and includes vocal affirmations that act as small positive rewards whenever you log calories today.

Calorie Tracker does not collect nor share any data. it’s designed with respect for your privacy. Its focus is adding value to your everyday lifestyle.
