Calorie Tracker - Privacy Policy

Cloud Mill Games
Effective date: 25/11/2026
Last updated: 25/11/2026

The short version

Calorie Tracker stores your information on your phone and nowhere else. We do not have servers, we do not have accounts, and the app has no internet permission. We cannot see your data, and neither can anyone else.

What information the app stores

The app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your sex, height, weight, target weight, age and activity level
  • Calorie entries you add and the daily limit calculated from them
  • Weight and activity check-ins you record
  • Your preferences: units, day reset time, notification choices, voice settings

This information is held in the app’s private storage. Other apps cannot read it, and we never receive a copy.

What we collect

Nothing. We collect no personal information, no usage statistics, no device identifiers and no crash reports. The app makes no network requests of any kind.

What the app does with your information

Everything the app calculates is done on your phone: BMI, your daily calorie limit, and the adjustments made at each check-in. Nothing is sent anywhere for processing.

Permissions, and why they exist

  • Notifications — to show the day-start reminder and your weekly check-in reminder. Granting this is optional; the app works fully without it.
  • Run at device startup — to restore your scheduled reminders after a restart. This does not start the app or collect anything.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, files, contacts, location or phone state, and it does not use an advertising identifier.

Voice encouragement

If you turn on voice encouragement, the app uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine to speak a short fixed phrase. The phrase is not recorded, and no audio is stored or transmitted. You can disable this at any time in Preferences.

Third parties

We share nothing, because we hold nothing and the app transmits nothing. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software.

Children

Calorie Tracker is not directed at children under 13. Because the app stores all data on the device and collects nothing, no information about any user is transmitted to us regardless of age.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently. There is no account to close and no server-side copy to request deletion of, so we cannot delete data on your behalf: we never had it.

Security

Your data sits in the app’s private storage area, protected by your device’s own security. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no transmission to intercept.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app begins collecting or transmitting anything, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@cloudmillgames.com
